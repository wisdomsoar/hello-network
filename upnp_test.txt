

1. PrPl DUT 需allow WAN private ip: 
   1.1. miniupnp-prpr的Makefile "ALLOW_RESERVED_ADDR" 要=y.
   1.2. ubus-cli UPnP.IGDConfig.AllowReservedAddr=1  > cat /var/etc/miniupnpd.conf "pf_allow_reserved_addr=yes".
   滿足以上兩點, WAN private ip 才會帶到封包裡<NewExternalIPAddress>, upnpc -s 有External IP後, 才能設定portmapping , 不然上述兩項沒開只有 public ip才會有External IP.
2. 封包裡<NewExternalIPAddress>需有IP:
   2.1. connected IGD (public WAN IP)可以設定 port mapping成功.
   2.2. not connected IGD (private WAN IP) 可以透過 -i 來設定 port mapping成功.
3. 透過LAN PC 執行 upnpc_static.exe -r 11661 tcp 11155 udp
